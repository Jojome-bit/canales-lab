Welcome to the Canales Lab

About Us

We are a perioperative research lab focused on brain health, frailty, and equity in older adults undergoing surgery. Our research aims to understand and prevent perioperative neurocognitive disorders, to identify frailty at the bedside using tools like point-of-care ultrasound, and to make cognitive screening work for every patient, including those who prefer languages other than English.

We use this knowledge to build better preoperative screening pathways, from telehealth geriatric assessment to equity-informed applications of new technology, and we translate our findings into clinical training through the UCLA Simulation Center.

Research

Perioperative Neurocognitive Disorders

Project Overview

Postoperative delirium and cognitive decline are among the most common complications facing older adults after surgery, yet patients are rarely counseled about them. We study who is at risk, what patients are told, and how to screen and diagnose these disorders, including recent work on preoperative cannabis use and postoperative delirium after major noncardiac surgery, and on delirium screening in the PACU.

Key publication: Information and Counseling Gaps on Perioperative Neurocognitive Disorders Among Older Adults (JAGS, 2025)

Frailty & Point-of-Care Ultrasound

Project Overview

Frailty predicts poor surgical outcomes, but measuring it at the bedside is hard. Our diagnostic accuracy study in Anesthesiology showed that preoperative point-of-care ultrasound of muscle can identify frailty and predict postoperative outcomes. We continue to develop practical, scalable tools to optimize older adults before the operating room.

Key publication: Preoperative Point-of-Care Ultrasound to Identify Frailty and Predict Postoperative Outcomes (Anesthesiology, 2022)

Language Equity in Diagnosis

Project Overview

Patients who prefer languages other than English are routinely excluded from perioperative cognitive research, so the tools we use were never validated for them. Through our prospective cohort work and Dr. Canales' National Academy of Medicine project, "Advancing Language Equity in Diagnosing Perioperative Neurocognitive Disorders," we are working to close that gap.

Key publication: Language Preference and Preoperative Cognitive Screening in Older Adults (Anesthesia & Analgesia, 2024)

Telehealth & Digital Screening

Project Overview

Many older adults face barriers to in-person preoperative evaluation. We are testing whether geriatric and cognitive screening can move out of the clinic without losing reliability, expanding access to comprehensive preoperative assessment for the patients who need it most.

Key publication: Assessing the Reliability of Telehealth Geriatric Screening in Older Adults Undergoing Surgery (Anesthesiology, 2026)

AI & Ethics in Perioperative Medicine

Project Overview

New technology can widen health disparities as easily as it can close them. From the ethics of big data to equity-informed frameworks for large language models in perioperative care, we examine how emerging tools should, and should not, shape anesthesia practice.

Key publication: Applying Large Language Models in Perioperative Medicine With an Equity-Informed Perspective (Anesthesia & Analgesia, 2026)

Simulation & Clinical Education

Project Overview

Dr. Canales serves as Medical Director of the UCLA Simulation Center, where our research is translated into training: virtual simulation cases on interpreter use for language-diverse patients, and curricula on humanism, empathy, and teamwork in anesthesiology.

Learn more on our Clinical Education page and at the UCLA Simulation Center

Clinical Education

Our lab believes research should change how clinicians are trained. Through the UCLA Simulation Center, we turn our findings on the aging brain, frailty, and language equity into hands-on education for the next generation of anesthesiologists.

Simulation-Based Training

Dr. Canales brings more than 18 years of simulation experience, including founding leadership of the UC Irvine simulation center, and now directs the UCLA Simulation Center serving students, residents, and faculty.

Principal Investigator

Cecilia Canales, MD, MPH, MS

Assistant Professor in Residence, Anesthesiology & Perioperative Medicine · Medical Director, UCLA Simulation Center

Dr. Canales is a physician-scientist whose work sits at the intersection of geriatric perioperative medicine, diagnostic excellence, and health equity. She received her MD from the UC Irvine School of Medicine, completed her anesthesiology residency at UCLA, and holds a Master of Public Health and a Master of Science in Clinical Research. Her research showed that preoperative point-of-care ultrasound can identify frailty and predict postoperative outcomes, and her current studies extend cognitive screening to telehealth and to patients who prefer languages other than English.

She is a National Academy of Medicine Scholar in Diagnostic Excellence, a UCLA CTSI Career Development Award recipient, and brings more than 18 years of experience in simulation-based education, including founding leadership of the UC Irvine simulation center. UCLA faculty profile →
